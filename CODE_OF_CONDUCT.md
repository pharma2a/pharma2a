# Code of conduct

PharmA2A adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
for everyone who takes part in the project: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
space where the project is represented.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, criticise ideas and not people, and keep
discussions about the protocol. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted.

Report unacceptable behaviour to contact@pharma2a.org. Reports are read by the maintainers
(see `MAINTAINERS.md`), handled in confidence, and answered within five working days. The
maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may
exclude a participant from the project.

*PharmA2A applique le Contributor Covenant 2.1 : respect, bonne foi, on critique les idées et
pas les personnes. Signalement : contact@pharma2a.org.*
